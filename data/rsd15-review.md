# RSD15 series reconciliation

Checked against the official publisher on 7 September 2026. Maintainer review for
[issue #3](https://github.com/antlerboy/systemsmap/issues/3). The submitted span
20 August to 2 November must not be accepted as a single calendar event.

## Dated records

- São Paulo: 20 August. Existing community-feed record TU2133404103 retained.
- [RSDX-ASC online](https://rsdsymposium.org/rsd15-cybernetics/): 24-27 August.
  Added as its own reviewed date-only record. It is distinct from ASC's 3-7 August
  meeting in Ouro Preto, which the publisher explicitly describes separately.
- [Paper talks](https://rsdsymposium.org/rsd15-paper-talks/): 8-9 October,
  online. Existing community-feed record TU2133404975 retained.
- [Turin](https://rsdsymposium.org/rsd15-turin/): 13-16 October, hub and
  livestream. Existing community-feed record TU2133405689 retained.
- Retreat in Ostana: 18-21 October. Existing community-feed record TU2133406596
  retained. The source is the official programme navigation at
  https://rsdsymposium.org/ .

These are five dated records, not a complete inventory of all individual sessions.
Existing event IDs and calendar UIDs are not replaced.

## Unresolved publisher inconsistencies

- [Communications perspectives](https://rsdsymposium.org/rsd15-cmmi/): the
  official page's navigation says 29 September, but its own programme heading
  says 28 September. Do not guess which is correct or insert both as two events.
- Shanghai is listed as September, date to be announced. No exact date is
  invented.
- [RSDX online](https://rsdsymposium.org/rsd15-rsdx-online/) says 'August to
  November' in its heading, but 'August through October' in the body. Mapping
  Mondays and Workshop Wednesdays describe a series, not a continuous booking.
  Dated individual sessions still need reconciliation against the online hub.

Issue #3 stays open for these date and session questions. This review does not
alter the anonymous D1 submission service, its redirects, or Tangle's pinned
interface integration.
